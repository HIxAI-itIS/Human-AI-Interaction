# Human-AI-Interaction
Human-centered AI interaction software focused on preserving meaning, context, and individuality in spoken and written communication.
## Personal motivation

My motivation for developing Human–AI Interaction extends beyond learning to work with artificial intelligence.

I want to use my professional knowledge, experience in human communication, and understanding of human behavior to make a meaningful contribution to the development of AI.

I believe that human intelligence and artificial intelligence can complement one another, particularly when technology is designed to recognize and respect the individuality of human thought, language, and expression.

Through this open-source project, I hope to translate my knowledge and ideas into practical software that can benefit others.

For me, this initiative represents an opportunity not only to develop new technical skills, but also to contribute to the future of human-centered artificial intelligence.
