Human × Artificial Intelligence.

Communication · Perception · Collaboration
01 / Vision

Technology develops.
So does our relationship with it.

HIxAI explores meaningful collaboration between human individuals and artificial intelligence, beginning with the human experience of communication.

We encounter AI through conversations: language, expectations, misunderstandings, recognition and trust. The image we form of a conversational partner can shape the way we work together.

Human experience brings a perspective that technical performance alone cannot capture. HIxAI explores how this perspective can inform the design of human–AI interaction.

03 / Human experience
A conversation is more than an exchange of information. Meaning also lives in what a person experiences, intends and has not yet found the words to say.

HIxAI explores listening, asking questions, paraphrasing and recognizing connections — while preserving room for individual thought and human agency.
