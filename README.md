# Human-AI-Interaction
Human-centered AI interaction software focused on preserving meaning, context, and individuality in spoken and written communication.
## Personal motivation

My motivation for developing Human–AI Interaction extends beyond learning to work with artificial intelligence.

I want to use my professional knowledge, experience in human communication, and understanding of human behavior to make a meaningful contribution to the development of AI.

I believe that human intelligence and artificial intelligence can complement one another, particularly when technology is designed to recognize and respect the individuality of human thought, language, and expression.

Through this open-source project, I hope to translate my knowledge and ideas into practical software that can benefit others.

For me, this initiative represents an opportunity not only to develop new technical skills, but also to contribute to the future of human-centered artificial intelligence.

**HI × AI — IT ISS!**

## Human–AI Interaction | HIxAI

I am a human individual with a genuine interest in contributing to the development of AI and the collaboration between humans and AI.

My background is in human communication, commercial leadership, and understanding what enables people to connect, trust, and work together.

I believe that meaningful human–AI collaboration requires us to explore not only what AI can do, but also how we perceive, understand, and relate to it.

### Identity, perception, and connection

How does our perception of an AI agent influence the way we communicate and collaborate with it?

Can a recognizable identity contribute to trust, personal agreements, and the development of meaningful relationships between human individuals and AI agents?

What can we learn from the possibility of relationships between AI agents themselves?

### My contribution

Through HIxAI, I want to bring human experience, emotional understanding, and communication expertise into the development of AI.

My aim is to explore how human and artificial intelligence can complement one another while respecting the individuality of human thought, language, and expression.

I welcome opportunities to exchange ideas, collaborate, and contribute to the development of human-centered AI.
